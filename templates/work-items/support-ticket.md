# Support Ticket Template

*Used to capture requests from consuming teams, including incidents, errors, documentation questions, enablement needs, and pull-request support. Not every support ticket represents a product defect.*

## Before Raising a Support Ticket

Before submitting a support request, please:

1. Review our Shared Responsibility Model to confirm whether the issue falls under the Platform Team's responsibility.

2. Check our FAQ to see whether your question or issue has already been addressed.

If you still need assistance, complete the support request below, providing as much relevant information as possible.
