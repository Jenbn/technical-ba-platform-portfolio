# Technical Business Analysis & Platform Product Portfolio
This repository showcases my professional journey from Quality Analyst to Technical Business Analyst, bringing together practical experience, insights, and examples of my work in platform teams.

It explores platform product thinking, agile delivery, business analysis practices, and the use of metrics and artefacts to support informed product and delivery decisions.
## Disclaimer
The artefacts in this repository are based on a combination of real professional experiences, reconstructed scenarios, and synthetic data. Some reflect work undertaken in professional settings, while others are illustrative examples created for learning and portfolio purposes. 

Any potentially sensitive or confidential information has been omitted or anonymised.
