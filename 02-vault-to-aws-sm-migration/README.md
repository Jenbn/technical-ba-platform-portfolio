# Vault to AWS Secrets Manager Migration

## Overview

This case study showcases my experience as a Business Analyst during the discovery and planning phases of a migration from HashiCorp Vault to AWS Secrets Manager within an internal platform team.

It focuses on **user research, cross-functional collaboration, and workshop facilitation**, demonstrating how user needs and feedback can inform migration planning and backlog creation.

## My Contribution

- **Stakeholder identification:** Identified seven stakeholder groups and recruited two representatives from each.
- **User interviews:** Prepared and conducted interviews in collaboration with UX designers, who documented the responses while I facilitated the conversations.
- **Research synthesis:** Collaborated with UX designers to review, categorise, and present findings to the platform team.
- **User-story mapping:** Prepared and facilitated a Mural workshop with the platform team, connecting user flows, pain points, and expectations to work items and release planning.
